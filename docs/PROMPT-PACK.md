# Prompt pack: look targets for the Junior low-poly Amazon game

For Aarush to run through his Claude + Higgsfield. Optional: the build does not wait for these.
The pictures are only looked at (by the builder and the blind critic). They are never put in the repository, the build or the link.

**Attach to every prompt:** the Eos references listed under it (real place: what things are) and two or three Junior kit study pictures of forests (shape language only; for example the `kit-biomes` jungle and forest pictures in the Junior library). Do not attach the old GPT pictures.

**Style line (paste at the end of every prompt):**
> chunky low-poly 3D game world, big flat faceted planes, flat colours from one small palette, no textures, no outlines; made older and richer with cinematic light: warm key light, cool coloured shadows, haze stacking the forest into near, middle and far layers; dense and layered but readable; third-person game camera behind a 12-year-old kid seen from behind, about six heads tall, simple face not shown; not cute, not toy-like, not photoreal, not dark and glowing; no text, no logos, no named characters.

## 1. Flooded forest at high water
Eos: AM117, AM127, AM091, HB003.
> A kid in a small wooden canoe paddles between tree trunks standing in calm brown flood water, deep inside a flooded Amazon forest. Pale and dark trunks with flared buttress roots, a clear darker band on every trunk up to the same height, hanging vines, palms in clumps, floating leaves and round green pads on the water, ripples around the paddle, a white bird low over the water, light shafts through the canopy, far trunks fading into green haze.

## 2. The channel at low water
Eos: A004, AM008, PW004.
> Late afternoon in the dry season: a narrow river channel sunk between bare mud banks taller than a house, cracked pale silt and driftwood on the slopes, roots hanging from the bank top, tall forest above with one umbrella-crowned giant, a kid walking the bank edge looking down at a small canoe on the brown water far below, warm low sun, long shadows.

## 3. The floating house at dusk
Eos: HB001, HB002, HB003.
> Dusk on a river channel: a small painted wooden house floating on a raft of big logs, its deck wider than the house, ropes running to trees on the bank, one warm lamp lit on the deck, a table, baskets, a canoe tied alongside, potted plants, a kid stepping from the canoe onto the deck, pink and violet sky reflected in brown water, dark forest behind.

## 4. The community on the high bank
Eos: AM128, AM096, PW001.
> Morning in a small river community on a high bank: wooden houses on tall stilts with tin roofs, plank walks and steps, a long flight of steps cut down the mud bank to a landing, a radio aerial pole, fan palms and fruit trees in a swept yard, a few people in everyday clothes at work, a kid seen from behind walking the plank walk, clear warm light, forest wall behind.

## 5. The giant tree
Eos: AM001, AM006, AM009.
> Inside the forest: one enormous tree wider than a house with buttress roots like walls snaking across the leaf-covered ground, a clear darker flood line well above head height on the trunk, a kid standing small at its foot looking up, big-leaved shrubs and ferns around, light shafts and floating dust in green haze, far trunks layered into the distance.

## 6. The lake at first light
Eos: AM014, AM005, A003.
> First light on a still forest lake: mist low on the water, a kid sitting in a canoe seen from behind, close in front of the canoe the broad dark back of a huge fish rolling at the surface with a ring spreading on the still water (only its back and tail tip, never the whole fish), round green pads near the shore, a wall of palms and pale trunks around the lake, the sun just rising orange behind an umbrella-crowned giant, sky going from grey-violet to gold.

## 7. Night by lamp
Eos: AM117, HB002.
> Night in the forest: a kid and an adult walking a narrow trail, lit only by one warm hand lamp, big leaves and trunks catching the light near them and falling to deep blue-black beyond, fireflies, a pale moonlit gap of sky above, mist over the ground. The picture is dark but every shape is readable.
