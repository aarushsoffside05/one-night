# UNSOURCED: what CHEIA needs that no cleared fact supports

For GAME-PLAN.md (CHEIA - Three Weeks of Rising Water). Replaces the earlier list whole.

Rule (brief section 7): anything here may be shown, kept visually plausible and generic, but no caption, label or on-screen text may state it as a fact about Mamirauá or its people. Where a person in the story says a line tagged with a U-number, it is that made-up person's practical reason for what they are doing at that moment, not a claim about how things are done in the reserve. A reader from the reserve's communities should see every item before the game is shown to anyone.

Cleared facts the plan DOES state are not listed here: the 10.60 m yearly swing, the months of high and low water, "the cheia", the reserve's area (S0213); the cassava rush, cattle fed cut foliage and kept in floating corrals, floating gardens, floating houses tied to trees with ropes checked as the water rises, palm leaves piled on stilt-house floors, travel by canoe including to school, paddling where one walked (S0311); trunks keep the flood mark, channels dry and lakes are cut off at low water (brief section 7, less firmly sourced); the pirarucu line at the scale of the Central Amazon Conservation Complex (S0203), once, on the closing map.
