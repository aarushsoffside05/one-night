# Unsourced

Things the build shows or says that are not on the brief's list of facts (section 7). Each is kept visually plausible and generic, and no claim is made about it in words unless noted. A reader from the reserve's own communities should check every line here.

## Said in words
- Scene 5, Lia: "We count them when they come up to breathe." Given in the brief as her family's own practice; its source is a wide one.

## Shown, not said
- How the forest looks: which shapes of tree, palm and plant, how dense, how tall. All are generic shapes made by rule. No plant is named anywhere.
- Buttress roots, hanging vines, hanging roots on the banks, floating plants at the water's edge, fallen wood stranded along the flood line.
- The house on legs: plank walls painted blue-green, a corrugated metal roof, a railed plank walk, steps, a water barrel, a bench.
- The floating house: its size, plank walls, palm-thatch roof, the fish table, crates, the lamp on a post, mooring posts, and the way its ropes run to trees on the bank.
- The bank steps and the small landing stage at their foot.
- The radio aerial: a pole with guy wires and a wire to the house.
- The marked tree: a painted ring on the trunk at last year's flood height.
- Small animals without names: birds crossing the sky (dark and white), butterflies (yellow, blue, white), fireflies at night, small fish as shadows under the surface. No animal is named except the giant fish, by Lia, at the very end.
- The colour of the water (brown by day), of the sand, mud and cracked silt.
- The flood mark on every trunk: a stain below 10.6 m above low water with a darker line at its top. The brief lists "tree trunks keep the mark of the flood" as less firmly sourced; how it looks here is invented.
- The lake sits 6.2 m above the low river and the forest floor about 8 m above it; the yard 8.8 m; the knoll 10.3 m. These heights are chosen so that the brief's numbers hold (10.60 m swing; forest flooded 1.90 to 4.65 m deep; the yard's line over a boy's head), not measured from the place.
