# UNSOURCED - CHEIA (Three Weeks of Rising Water)

Everything the game plan needs, shows or has a person say that no cleared fact supports. Cleared facts are only the brief's section 7 list and the facts in `02-truth/2026-11-13-facts.md` whose Place is Mamirauá (S0213, S0311; S0203 once, at region scale). Each entry below: what it is, where it appears, how it is kept generic. None of these may be stated as a fact about the place in any caption, card, map or about-panel. Captions that touch them carry the U-number in `GAME-PLAN.md`.

**Ask the community reader first about U8 and U9** (one family's cow and calf on a floating corral inside the reserve, and moving it).

## Things deliberately NOT in the game because nothing clears them
- Any name for a plant, a fish, a bird or any animal in a caption or on the line (only the closing map's one region-scale line).
- Any statement that the big fish comes up to breathe, or is counted or fished.
- Any fishing, net, hook, or caught fish on screen.
- Any month for "today", any rate of rise, any depth in metres or centimetres on screen.
- Any belief, legend, rite, saying presented as traditional, costume or body paint; any named people or named community.
- Any claim about how cattle are loaded onto or off a corral (the cow is already aboard when first seen and stays aboard).
