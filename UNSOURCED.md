# UNSOURCED: what the game plan needs that no cleared fact supports

Rule (brief, section 7): anything not on the brief's list or a Mamirauá fact is kept visually plausible and generic, makes no claim in words where that can be avoided, and is listed here. A reader from the reserve's own communities must see these before the game is shown to anyone.

Each entry: what it is, where it appears in `GAME-PLAN.md`, how it is kept generic, and what to do if a reader says it is wrong. Numbers match the `[U-n]` tags on captions.

## C. General statements that are not about the place (listed for the reader's eye)

These are plain facts of light, water and weight, said as the physical reason for an action. They make no claim about Mamirauá.

- "I cannot work blind." / "With a lamp in our eyes we cannot see the water." (glare)
- "He cannot see his own feet." / "I can't see past the canoe." (a load blocks the view)
- "Lift early and it slides off my shoulder." (two people, one load)
- "Two canoes drift apart. Held, they are one raft."
- "Water lies flat. The ground doesn't."

## D. Deliberately not in the game

Not shown and not said, because no cleared fact supports them here: any named animal or plant in play; caimans; fish counts, quotas or rules; how a net is made or set; what the fish is sold for; raised floors; families moving to high ground; school stopping in floods; cassava; cattle; assemblies; any belief, story of the past, greeting, song or custom; any real community's name.
