# Hero models (headless Blender)

Everything in `src/assets/models/*.glb` is generated by the Python in this folder with Blender as a
Python module (`bpy` 5.3.0). Nothing is downloaded: every mesh is lofted from rings/boxes in code,
there are no textures, no UVs, no materials, no add-on assets.

| script | what it does |
| --- | --- |
| `build_models.py` | builds all meshes and writes `boats.glb`, `fish.glb`, `people.glb` |
| `verify_models.py` | parses the GLB JSON/binary by hand and re-imports into Blender; checks names, identity transforms, attributes, sizes, triangle limits, pure mask colours, total size. Exits non-zero on failure |
| `preview.py OUT_DIR [filter…]` | imports the shipped `.glb` files, assembles test figures with the joint numbers below and renders check stills (Cycles, CPU, 20 samples) |

```sh
PY=/path/to/venv-with-bpy/bin/python      # pip install bpy==5.3.0
$PY tools/blender/build_models.py          # ~1 s
$PY tools/blender/verify_models.py         # must print "ALL CHECKS PASSED"
$PY tools/blender/preview.py /tmp/model-previews            # all stills, ~60 s on 4 cores
$PY tools/blender/preview.py /tmp/model-previews figure fish  # only shots whose name matches
```

(`import bpy` must come before `import bmesh` / `mathutils`. The `__pycache__` folder that Python
drops here is not needed.)

## File conventions (as implemented and verified)

- glTF 2.0 binary, exported with Blender's stock exporter, `export_yup=True`. Units are metres.
- In the file: **+Y up, front = +Z, left/right = X**. The geometry is authored in exactly these
  coordinates in `build_models.py`.
- Every mesh is its own node, node name = mesh name, **no translation/rotation/scale on any node**
  (geometry is in final coordinates relative to the pivot).
- One primitive per mesh, attributes `POSITION`, `NORMAL`, `COLOR_0` only. Indexed triangles.
  No `TEXCOORD_0`, no materials, no images/textures/samplers, no skins, no animations, no cameras,
  no Draco.
- `COLOR_0` is `VEC4`, unsigned short, normalized; alpha is always 1. Values are **linear** (as glTF
  specifies), i.e. the colours were chosen as sRGB hex values and converted to linear before being
  stored, so three.js with the usual sRGB output shows the intended paint colours.
- Shading is smooth with hard edges where the angle between faces exceeds a per-mesh threshold
  (35–70°); the exporter writes those as split normals.
- All closed parts are wound outwards (checked per connected island at build time).

## boats.glb — vertex colour is the final painted colour

- **`canoe`** 5.20 × 0.80 m, plank-built river canoe. Origin = centre of the boat on the waterline.
  Hull bottom y = −0.13, gunwale y = +0.23 amidships rising to +0.38 at the bow tip (+Z) and +0.34
  at the stern tip. Real wall thickness (28 mm) with a rub rail, visible darker inside, 2 plank
  seats (top at y = +0.125, centred at z = −1.05 and z = +0.72), 4 ribs. Each strake has its own
  tone with crisp seams between strakes; darker/greyer near the waterline, lighter worn gunwale.
- **`canoe_long`** 7.60 × 1.00 m, same construction and same origin rule, 5 benches (top y = +0.125,
  at z = −2.29, −1.23, −0.17, +0.90, +1.96), 6 ribs.
- **`paddle`** 1.45 m. Origin at the middle of the shaft. Shaft along Y; grip top at y = +0.525;
  blade (0.20 wide, 0.40 long, round leaf with a small tip) from y = −0.525 to −0.925, blade faces ±Z.
- **`surfboard`** 1.75 × 0.48 m, 0.06 m thick at the centre. Origin at the centre of the underside
  (bottom is y = 0 there). Nose at +Z with 0.075 m of rocker (top of nose y ≈ +0.09), tail lifts
  0.02. Three fins under the tail reach y = −0.082. Cream, one broad teal stripe and a thin orange
  pinstripe run lengthwise on the deck (they taper with the outline; hard colour edges).
- **`skiff`** 6.20 × 1.50 m aluminium boat. Origin centre on the waterline: z = −3.10 is the outer
  face of the square transom, z = +3.10 the bow tip. Bottom y = −0.18 (shallow V, hard chine),
  gunwale y = +0.42 from the transom to amidships rising to +0.51 at the bow. 30 mm walls, 3 bench
  seats (top y = +0.22, centres z = −2.27, −0.50, +1.18), small foredeck plate. Dull grey-green
  with a faded red-orange stripe under the gunwale, scuff patches.
- **`outboard`** 1.10 m tall. Origin = transom clamp point. To mount on the skiff put it at
  **(0, 0.42, −3.085)** (transom top edge, mid-thickness). Cowl top y = +0.43, skeg bottom
  y = −0.67, propeller at z ≈ −0.39…−0.42, y = −0.52; tiller arm reaches forward to z = +0.44 at
  y ≈ +0.24. Dark grey cowl with a red band.

## fish.glb

- **`fish`** stylised pirarucu, 2.30 m, head toward +Z, back = +Y, origin at the body centre.
  The body is a tube of **22 rings evenly spaced every 0.10 m from z = −0.95 to z = +1.15**, 12
  vertices per ring (odd rings are rotated half a step so the vertices form a scale-like lattice).
  Tail fin z = −0.90…−1.14; dorsal fin z = −0.25…−0.98 and anal fin z = −0.35…−0.98 have a ring
  every 0.10 m too, so a bend that displaces vertices as a function of z bends everything together.
  Pectoral fins at z ≈ +0.39…+0.60, small pelvic fins at z ≈ −0.12…−0.25.
  Colour: dark olive head and back, pale belly, large scales hinted with dark centres and red edges
  that strengthen toward the tail (rear third red), dark-red fins, yellow-ringed eyes, dark mouth line.

## people.glb — a kit of rigid parts; vertex colours are MASKS

| mask value | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| (1,0,0) red | skin |
| (0,1,0) green | main clothing colour of that part (shirt on `torso` and sleeve of `upperarm`; shorts/skirt on `hips_*` and `thigh_shorts`; **the hat's own colour on `hat_cap` / `hat_straw`**) |
| (0,0,1) blue | hair |
| (0,0,0) black | dark facial features (eyes, mouth) |
| (1,1,1) white | light detail: band of `hat_straw`, top button of `hat_cap` |

Only these five exact values occur (verified); boundaries are hard (vertices are split there).
A tint in a shader: `col = r*skin + g*cloth + b*hair`, with white = `min(r,g,b)` → light colour,
black stays dark.

What each part contains:

- `torso` green, small red collar/neck stub on top · `hips_shorts`, `hips_skirt` all green
- `upperarm` green sleeve from the shoulder down to y = −0.095, red below · `forearm` red (mitten
  hand, thumb hint toward +Z)
- `thigh` all red · `thigh_shorts` (extra, same pivot and length) green shorts leg down to
  y = −0.25, red below · `shin` red, with bare foot
- `head` red with black eyes and mouth, soft nose bump, tiny ears, short neck
- `hair_short`, `hair_long`, `hair_bun`, `hair_tuft` all blue · `hat_cap` green + white button ·
  `hat_straw` green + white band

### Joint / pivot numbers for the 1.70 m reference adult (copy these)

Each part's origin is its joint pivot. Part-local frame: +Y up, +Z front. Figure root on the
ground between the feet.

| quantity | value (m) |
| --- | --- |
| waist (pelvis point) height above the soles | **0.94** |
| `torso` pivot | waist + (0, 0, 0) |
| `hips_shorts` / `hips_skirt` pivot | waist + (0, 0, 0) |
| `head` (and every hair/hat) pivot = neck base | waist + (0, **0.50**, 0) |
| shoulder joints (`upperarm` pivots) | waist + (**±0.18**, **0.45**, 0) |
| elbow (`forearm` pivot) | upperarm pivot + (0, **−0.28**, 0) |
| hand tip | forearm pivot + (0, −0.37, 0) (wrist at −0.24) |
| hip joints (`thigh` pivots) | waist + (**±0.09**, **−0.07**, 0) |
| knee (`shin` pivot) | thigh pivot + (0, **−0.42**, 0) |
| sole | shin pivot + (0, **−0.45**, 0); ankle at −0.39; foot z −0.062…+0.168, 0.09 wide |
| top of skull | head pivot + (0, 0.30, 0) |

Check: soles = 0.94 − 0.07 − 0.42 − 0.45 = **0.00**; top of head = 0.94 + 0.50 + 0.30 = **1.74**;
shoulders at y = 1.39, elbows 1.11, hand tips 0.74, hips 0.87, knees 0.45, neck base 1.44.
The same numbers are in `RIG` in `build_models.py`, and `preview.py` assembles its figures from them.

The same mesh is used for left and right limbs (no mirroring needed; parts are symmetric in X).
Other characters are made by scaling the whole figure (the child in the previews is 0.72).

Head-local landmarks (for look-at, attaching things): head centre (0, 0.166, 0.008), eyes at
(±0.041, 0.171, 0.115), mouth y ≈ 0.09, chin y ≈ 0.032, ears x = ±0.113.

## Decisions

- **Hip joint offset is −0.07, not −0.14.** With the requested segment lengths (0.42 + 0.45) and
  the requested waist height 0.94 this puts the soles exactly on y = 0 and the head top at 1.74, so
  the waist height, all limb lengths and the torso/head numbers stay exactly as specified.
- **Parts overlap on purpose** so joints never open: torso reaches 0.034 below the waist and 0.008
  above the neck base, hips start 0.026 above the waist, limbs have rounded ends that stick
  0.03–0.05 past their pivots, the neck plugs 0.04 into the torso.
- **`hips_shorts` is short boxer-style** (ends at y = −0.20, i.e. 0.13 below the hip joints), and
  its leg stubs are rigid with the pelvis. With the plain red `thigh` this reads as short shorts.
  For knee-ish shorts use the extra **`thigh_shorts`** instead of `thigh` (green to 0.25 below the
  hip, tinted with the same colour as the hips). It was not in the brief; drop it if unwanted.
  Under `hips_skirt` either thigh works (it is hidden).
- **Hats use green for their own colour**, so tint a hat mesh with the hat colour rather than the
  shirt colour.
- `hat_cap` (shell 24 mm off the skull) and `hat_straw` fit over the bare head, `hair_short` and
  `hair_bun` (13 mm). `hair_tuft` (21 mm + spikes) and `hair_long` poke through the hats; do not combine.
- `hair_long` hangs to y = −0.015 (just below the neck base) so it clears the shoulders at rest; it
  is rigid with the head and will clip the shoulders slightly on big head turns.
- No eyebrows (kept simpler); nose is a 7 mm soft bump; ears are 12 mm bumps.
- Head is 0.21 wide, 0.23 deep and 0.27 tall chin-to-crown (0.30 with neck) so it is a little
  large for the body, as asked.
- Boat lengths/beams are measured over everything (rub rail and raked stems included), so the
  bounding boxes are exactly the requested sizes.
- Canoe seams use per-strake corner colours (about 100 extra vertices per canoe) to get a planked
  look without textures; everything else is smooth per-vertex colour.
- The fish's 12-sided rings are staggered on odd rings to make the scale lattice. Ring spacing
  is still exactly 0.10 m.
- Surfboard stripes run lengthwise and follow the outline, rather than being constant-width.
- Triangle counts are well under the limits (skiff 513 of 1600, fish 756 of 1400) to keep the
  download small; the three files total 255 KB.

## Known weak spots

- Figures are slim and stiff-shouldered (sleeves read as slightly puffed caps); elbows and knees
  are plain overlapping capsules and will show a crease, not a gap, when bent far.
- The skiff is plain: flat panels, no ribs or rivets. The scuffing is subtle.
- The outboard's propeller is three flat blades, only a hint.
- The fish's mouth and eyes are thin raised decals (4–5 mm proud of the surface).
- Hair and hat shells are open underneath (hidden inside the head); they are not meant to be seen
  without the head.
